This same method could be used for infinite ammo, as well, without the needs of having bazillions of items in the closet
Basically it uses a infinitely scrolling crusher, that activates a scrolling floor while moving... you have start and stop crusher lines to activate the "healing" floor, but starting and stopping the scrolling. In the closet is a helper deco_thing that has shootable and touchy.. on death it spawns a healing pack, and then the next frame a copy of itself. ... upshot is that whatever pick up you want to spawn is dropped when the voodoo doll collides with the tool. Spawning the tool on a moving doll will activate touchy, but if it is not moving it will not.. so upshot is.. activate scrolling floor, player gets pickup item, stop scrolling floor, stop picking up item. By adjusting the spawn frame times you can adjust the power of the effect.
